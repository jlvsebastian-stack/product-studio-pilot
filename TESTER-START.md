# Try Kaya Ko Yan! Digital Product Creator — Beta 0.3.21

Send this to Codex or another AI assistant with local computer tools:

> Install https://github.com/jlvsebastian-stack/product-studio-pilot on my computer, set up its launcher, and open Product Studio.

Next time, say **Launch Product Studio**. A regular website chat without local tools cannot install or launch software on your computer. Node.js 22 or newer is required; the installation instructions tell the local assistant how to handle setup.

## First test

1. Choose your own AI account and an available model. Start with ChatGPT/Codex on Windows, the tested path. Mac and live Claude are experimental in this beta. Your subscription allowance applies.
2. Bring one real skill and one specific buyer problem. The AI should help research comparable offers and contribute useful suggestions without inventing your experience or promising sales.
3. Keep the first product small. Approve the outline, expanded plan and one representative sample. For presentations, choose a template and review the three actual Canva sample slides. Build the remaining full draft, then review the complete product once.
4. Ask for one small correction. Confirm it changes the requested part and preserves the rest. Inspect the final quality review and its useful improvement suggestions.
5. Save project, reload, then use Open project. Your conversation and work should remain. Normal Save creates no Downloads file; Download a backup is the optional portable JSON copy.
6. Open the actual finished Google Docs, Google Sheets or Canva link when connected, or inspect the local PDF/text fallback. Test any spreadsheet with a simple input change and check its totals. A PDF is not a functioning spreadsheet.

Allow about an hour for a focused first draft; this is a test target, not a guarantee. Native files currently require the ChatGPT/Codex provider and your own connected Google/Canva accounts. Claude native delivery is not implemented. Missing connections should be explained without changing your selected provider.

Also try an explicit preference such as “Use plain English and short steps.” Check Settings / your preferences, edit or forget it, and confirm an inferred preference needs your permission.

## Tell us what happened

Report your operating system, AI provider, approximate time, installation friction, anything confusing, and whether the finished file is useful enough for its intended buyer. For a problem, include what you clicked, what you expected and the visible error; a screenshot is helpful. Do not post private interviews, project backups, credentials or customer information to the public repository. Share only details you choose.
