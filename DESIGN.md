# Presentation design contract

## Status and purpose

The local 0.3.16 candidate adds template choice before three custom sample slides and keeps webinar and presentation slides to fewer than15 total visible words, Title Case titles and source-grounded explanatory graphics. The three directions remain. The earlier30–34-word studies need refinement in text density, imagery and composition; passing checks do not establish aesthetic acceptance.

The local app guides creation and shows progress. Real presentations are reviewed and edited through external Canva links. Keep this project separate from KKY. The app interface, Josh's presentation identity and each creator's product identity are separate.

These rules apply to webinar and presentation slides only. Ebooks, guides, workbooks, templates and spreadsheets use their own craft rules, density and layout conventions. A mixed-format course applies the appropriate rules to each component.

## Required journey

1. Reuse the known audience, outcome, outline and explicit preferences. Offer one easy opportunity to supply a brand/style reference; do not start another questionnaire. If none is supplied, propose suitable directions.
2. Show **three distinct lightweight template previews** without custom AI generation. Vary typography, composition and imagery treatment; label these as template examples. The creator chooses ONE template, then the app generates exactly THREE source-specific sample slides only in that style. Do not automatically add a cover.
3. Explain each template's feel and audience fit briefly. Store the creator's explicit choice in this project. A choice authorizes sample creation, not automatic approval of those samples or a global profile preference.
4. Review all three actual sample slides, including a demanding comparison, process or demonstration. Several planned pages may teach different aspects of one canonical source section; preserve that full source and its notes on each mapped page. Obtain approval of the samples' look and content before expanding.
5. Build the rest using the chosen concept's page families. Inspect the actual Canva pages and PDF, then return a verified editable link with native per-slide notes. PDF/text remain honest fallbacks when native creation is unavailable.

Keep concept artifacts separate from the protected manuscript. Exploring looks does not authorize curriculum changes, invented expertise or rewriting approved content. Use the same source across concepts for a fair comparison.

## What defines a concept

Record a compact visual system: audience fit; references and the qualities to learn from; available heading/body fonts; color roles and hex values; imagery/illustration treatment; spacing/alignment; and compatible page families. Translate references into original work rather than copying another creator's identity, content or results.

Make the title/body hierarchy obvious, with readable line lengths and deliberate weights. Choose sizes for the slide's purpose and viewing conditions; a fixed 2.5× ratio is a starting suggestion, not a universal pass/fail rule. Muted secondary copy must remain readable on its actual background. Use at least 4.5:1 contrast for normal text and 3:1 for large text as a practical baseline.

Use a restrained set of color roles, plus useful tints/shades and appropriate imagery. Exactly three literal colors and a blanket ban on saturated colors are not requirements. Maintain **at least 40% empty space on every slide**, as the creator explicitly requires. Count quiet background outside text, graphics and substantive panels; a decorative panel does not make crowded content empty. Arrange the remaining content into a deliberate focal composition. Whitespace is one constraint, not evidence of beauty by itself: the rejected continuation failed as an entire composition. Left-align explanatory copy by default; a short centered statement may be intentional.

## Visuals and page families

Use the common `skills/product-page-design/references/slide-art-direction.md` contract and three runtime-loaded variations: `presentation-editorial` (fine ink and restrained washes), `presentation-studio` (bold mechanism-led compositions), and `presentation-warm` (tactile object scenes). These adapt Rizza's source → slide purpose → visual idea → specific prompt → individual composition → representative review process, rather than her brand or likeness.

Every slide records its own purpose, visual idea, native graphics plan and composition. Record a prompt and real asset ID only when delivered raster artwork is needed. An unchanged slide may reuse its saved asset on reopening/export; different slides cannot reuse that hero image. Default to fewer than 15 visible words including the title and every label, with an absolute 14-word ceiling. Keep the canonical source untouched, and retain its full original audience wording and original notes in the private plan and native presenter notes. Concise canvas copy is an explicit proposed adaptation for visual review.

Titles use Title Case while preserving acronyms and brand names. Each graphic represents or explains the content: record two to four exact source anchors and how they become visible features, and include that mapping in the real generation prompt. A reusable decorative symbol is insufficient. Match actual image aspect ratio and background to the composition; measure the contained picture rather than its empty outer slot. Prefer substantial imagery around20–35% of the canvas and reject a postage-stamp image below12%. Full rendered geometry is checked before spending an image generation request, with one bounded planning correction.

Choose the composition by teaching job: cover, statement, image-led explanation, comparison, process/journey, real demonstration, exercise or recap. One universal two-column master is insufficient. Do not rotate archetypes mechanically.

Every slide needs a focal point and deliberate composition. Use substantial topic-appropriate imagery, illustration, annotated demonstrations or crafted diagrams when they communicate the idea. Generic AI motifs, random decoration and another set of boxes do not replace art direction. A text-led statement slide is valid when intentional.

Use genuine assets and record their source, availability and intended use. Generated imagery must actually exist. Conceptual interfaces are illustrative, not verified working screenshots. Prefer fonts/assets the tester can use without another paid plan. Keep audience text editable rather than baking it into a full-slide raster. Keep explanations in presenter notes. Preserve approved wording; propose shortening instead of silently deleting it.

## Structured content and Canva production

Maintain a slide plan with a stable ID, purpose, headline, key message, approved audience copy, visual brief, layout family, asset references, presenter notes and source/claim references. Metrics are optional and require supplied or verified evidence. Never invent percentages, revenue, TAM or results to fill a decorative field.

Map that plan to the selected native Canva page family's actual fields. A comparison might use Choice_1_Label and Choice_1_Body; a journey needs its own step fields. Adding Layout_Family to a CSV does not itself select or draw a different master.

Bulk Create is an optional production adapter **after** concept and masters exist. Use an actual CSV, Canva Sheet or manual table. Inspect the selected design's dataset, match field names/types and preview a small fill before generating the rest. A Markdown table in chat is a planning view, not automatically an imported CSV. Confirm each tester's feature eligibility; do not make paid automation the only path.

Native-note writing and readback are separate. A Speaker_Notes column does not establish that notes were attached to the right pages. Verify real editability, order, assets, overflow and notes in the destination editor. This chat's connected tools do not establish a connection inside every tester's localhost app.

## Acceptance and rejection

Inspect actual rendered pages at normal size and as a deck overview against the chosen reference. Check type, composition, imagery, coherence and rhythm alongside accuracy, readability, bounds, source retention, editability and notes. A layout tag, successful export or passing tests cannot establish beauty.

The creator must be able to distinguish the concepts and recognize the selected direction in real content. If the look is rejected, record why and change direction; repeated recoloring is not a new concept. Use one bounded visual correction batch, then seek a concrete reference/direction if still misaligned. Do not expand an unaccepted sample.

No document guarantees every future design will be beautiful. These gates prevent a known rejected result from being treated as finished.

## Enforcement and current limits

AGENTS.md governs development. The runtime reads its loaded interview/craft/design skills and response/approval rules; it does not automatically read this document. The compact choose-template/three-sample/40% contract is loaded through the design skill and interview instructions. The application persists the chosen concept and visual approval, and the server holds new presentation sections until that approval matches the representative source. Changing that source requires another visual review; appending lessons retains the chosen look.

The actual illustrated plans and Canva transfer share one composition renderer, self-hosted fonts, editable text and separate graphic assets. Small cached image previews retain their truthful dimensions; original Canva MEDIA IDs are used for the native artwork fills. The explicit Create in Canva action authorizes the exact selected draft's validated import, original-art fills and save, without a repeated tool wall or local mockup approval. Verify persisted page/frame/media mapping, native metadata, audience text and per-slide notes before showing a completed editor link. Human visual approval follows through that actual link. Verified links persist with their exact source, concept and production and are labeled earlier drafts after a change. Native Claude delivery, Google Workspace creation and other people's installations remain separate unverified capabilities. Local PDF/text fallbacks retain their own layout and are labeled accordingly.

Keep plans and artwork privately on the creator's computer. Reopening unchanged work reuses existing assets. An explicit resume may continue saved plans only when incomplete generations were never accepted and have no native job ID. An accepted request with an unknown outcome is held for verification rather than silently creating another image. Human approval waiting time does not consume the bounded AI execution timeout.

Before export, independently check source-block completeness, source order, exact notes and actual native text-line extents. A specialized layout must consume the complete supported source pattern; otherwise reflow all blocks within bounded pages. Do not let a native readback verify an already-truncated expectation. Check the creator's exact selected ChatGPT model/quality before production and pass those values without substitution.

## Native Sample Review

For presentations and webinars, choose one of three lightweight template directions, then create exactly three real editable Canva sample slides with native presenter notes. Review those actual slides through their verified Canva editor link and obtain the creator's visual approval before expansion. The app shows progress and one persistent current-action bar; do not ask the creator to search for scattered confirmations.

Do not generate raster images to simulate a Canva preview. Preserve the selected illustrated direction: reuse current original slide assets, prepare only missing final-use artwork and then create the three real Canva samples automatically. Native text and useful geometry remain editable beside those separate original images. Do not silently swap approved illustrations for an icon-only deck or insert a local aesthetic approval stage. The fixed current-action bar leads to the verified Canva editor link for human sample review. Keep source-specific visual planning, varied composition, Title Case titles, at most14 visible words and at least40% empty space. Source and full original notes remain intact.

Before writing a new artwork prompt, resolve the learner's question and visual takeaway from the headline clues, full source and notes. Map two to four exact source anchors into concrete objects, actions and relationships; describe what the viewer must understand, not just what props are present. Alternatives remain independent, decisions branch and genuine processes show order. Prompt the exact relationship and exclusions, with no letters, numbers, invented metrics or fake working interface inside the art. A picture that could illustrate an unrelated slide unchanged fails this brief. The three variations change visual treatment and composition while keeping that same semantic discipline; Rizza's process is adapted without her personal branding.

The explicit Create in Canva action authorizes only that private draft's validated import and necessary edits/save. Routine steps need no repeated tool confirmation. This never grants sharing, publishing, unrelated design access or human aesthetic approval. A verified draft link is ready for review; the creator separately approves its look. Unknown accepted creation outcomes must be reconciled, never blindly duplicated.
