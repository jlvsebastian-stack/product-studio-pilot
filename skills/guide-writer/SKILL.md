# Guide Writer

## Planning Contract

For outline planning only, preserve the approved task sequence. Identify each step's starting material, concrete action, completed result and important recovery. Locate promised copy-ready tools and the end-to-end example within those units. Do not draft the guide, add chapters or ask for more intake.

## Production Contract

A guide helps the buyer complete a task. State the useful result, who the guide is for and the essential starting requirements. Organize the approved sections in the order the reader will use them.

For each meaningful step, give the actual action, explain the creator's judgment behind it, show a concrete example and identify what successful completion looks like. Connect the examples where useful so the reader can follow one task from beginning to end. Include the promised copy-ready tools rather than asking the buyer to invent them.

Explain the likely sticking point and a practical recovery or alternative. Distinguish a required step from an optional improvement. Finish with a short completion checklist the reader can use against their own result.

Choose the useful reading path from the already-known audience. A beginner tutorial demonstrates one complete task; a how-to gets a reader with the basics to one result; a reference exposes exact choices and limits. Do not blend long background explanations into the middle of an urgent procedure. Put the normal path first and exceptions immediately where they change an action.

Make instructions executable in everyday language: name the starting material, tell the reader what to change, show the resulting filled-in example and state what they should see or have next. For a copy-ready message or worksheet promised in the approved plan, provide the whole usable item with only necessary reader fields. Do not substitute "write an email," "research options" or a generic tip for the artifact the buyer paid to receive.

Use a compact decision table when the creator's method branches. Give the triggering condition, recommended action and reason; do not bury a meaningful exception in motivational prose. A worked example must reach the guide's promised end state, not stop after preparation.

Before returning new content, follow its shortest path as the intended reader using only the guide. Fix missing prerequisites, dangling references, conflicting numbers and unrecognizable success states during this same pass. Keep internal review notes out of the product; no extra interview or approval is needed.

Keep this a usable guide, without imposing book chapters, presentation limits or a quiz after every instruction. Use the creator's real method and label illustrative examples. Follow the approved outline and authorized edit boundaries; this skill adds no interview or approval steps.

Reference basis: [h4x3rotab's technical-docs-writing skill](https://gist.github.com/h4x3rotab/5a08422abad6093359da2c696a97a265) informs reader-task structure, content-type choice, complete examples, expected outputs and recovery. These are original adaptations for creator products; do not import its technical audit scoring or developer command workflows into the buyer's guide.

## Visual Design

<!-- Modified 2026-11-02: design guidance adapted from Anthropic skills (Apache-2.0), pinned at ca45fc496a3436251f191354091d557da31c15b5. See THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md. -->

- Design the reader’s shortest usable path with clear task headings, readable instructions and outcome checks. Choose typography and palette for this buyer’s situation, using the creator’s references.
- Keep a step, its example and its expected result visually connected. Use diagrams or genuine screenshots only when they clarify an actual action; do not invent interfaces or replace instructions with decorative icons.
- Inspect real Doc/PDF pages for readable hierarchy, intact steps and usable comparison tables. Retain enough explanation for independent use; slide density rules do not apply.

## Final Quality Review

- Follow the buyer's shortest end-to-end path using only the guide: inputs, actions, completed examples, promised tools and recovery must reach its useful result.
- Inspect actual rendered titles, headings, tables and pagination; correct safe clipping, isolated headings and accidental sparse continuation pages without rewriting approved instructions.
- Report missing dependencies or substantive gaps rather than inventing facts. Keep optional improvements tied to a specific reader difficulty and follow the shared review boundaries.
