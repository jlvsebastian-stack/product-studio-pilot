# Complete practical workbooks

## Planning Contract

For outline planning only, preserve the approved units and order. Name each useful exercise output, the teaching and completed example needed, its blank response surface and answer or success criteria. Describe the actual reusable workbook rather than a list of reflection questions; add no exercises outside scope.

## Production Contract

Use the creator's accumulated interview, approved buyer and niche, offer, outline, language, tone, and design direction. Do not copy anyone else's personal details, voice, brand, testimonials, or results. Shared teaching and layout methods are reusable; personalization comes only from the current creator.

Write to the workbook's reader. The workbook must stand on its own without access to the creator's AI conversation. A section should help the reader make a decision, produce something, diagnose a situation, or practice a skill.

Across the workbook, include these where they help the buyer complete the task; they are not a repeated five-part template for every section:

- Enough explanation of the creator's method, choices, and limits to use the exercise.
- Clear steps with enough detail to act, including relevant exceptions and common mistakes.
- A fully worked example with reasoning and a completed result, reused across connected exercises when helpful. Use a supplied case or label an illustrative case; never invent creator experience.
- An exercise with a concrete output, usable worksheet prompts, and adequate writing space.
- A useful result check or next action when it changes what the buyer does; omit routine reflection filler.

Let content determine length. Remove repeated introductions, motivational filler, hollow advice, and generic summaries. Add useful explanations when needed; do not pad to a page count. Avoid making every section the same exercise with different nouns.

Give each exercise a clear job and a usable response surface: an output to produce, brief instructions, the inputs or options needed, and labeled fields or a table sized for the expected answer. A paragraph telling the reader to "reflect" is not a worksheet. Use fill-in-the-blank items for a genuine recall or vocabulary task; use a decision worksheet, sorting task, calculation or plan when the buyer needs to apply judgment. Do not force every workbook into a school test.

Separate an illustrative completed example from the reader's blank version so it is obvious which to use. Put answer keys or model results after the attempt, including the reasoning and important acceptable alternatives. For personal or open-ended decisions, provide success criteria and a useful model response instead of inventing one correct personal answer. Never use blank lines as a substitute for teaching the method.

In the same generation pass, work through the new exercises using only their supplied instructions and inputs. Check that answer options match their items, calculations reconcile, referenced fields exist, and the result is reusable in the next exercise. Repair the content before returning it; this is an internal quality check, not a new creator task or approval gate.

Draft one representative section first and obtain explicit sample approval. Build full draft then completes all remaining approved units for one whole-product review; ordinary chat adds only the authorized next unit. Keep approvedDraft an exact unchanged prefix unless revisionIntent explicitly requests a revision, including its whitespace. Limit a requested revision to its scope; the UI preserves an undo checkpoint. Ask only for a genuinely blocking detail and explain which instruction, example or exercise it changes. Personal anecdotes are optional; label illustrative cases and separate proposed improvements from the creator's confirmed method.

Apply the selected language consistently. Preserve natural phrases and depth; do not force a polished generic AI voice. Use descriptive headings and plain text that the layout engine can style. The final introduction and instructions address the reader, not the prototype, JSON, approval stages, or generation mechanics.

Design for the real use: readable hierarchy, coherent typography and palette, generous exercise space, suitable print margins, restrained helpful graphics, and complete editable text. A cover and empty ruled pages do not establish a finished workbook. Check actual exported pages and content parity across formats. Export status must reflect what was generated and checked.

Use ## for each main section and ### for its teaching/example/exercise parts. A representative sample contains one main section. Where useful, add matching heading/layout/purpose metadata in brief.designPlan for teaching, example, exercise, checklist, summary or steps layouts; the manuscript remains the complete authority. Use safe built-in diagrams or a creator-supplied image when supported; never claim an unproduced image or native cloud design exists.

Finish with a useful next action. A complete file is ready for the creator's sale review; it is not proof of market demand, legal clearance, platform approval, or sales.

Reference basis: the Fill in the Blank Worksheets Generator and Educational Content Creator entries in the supplied [Awesome ChatGPT Prompts archive](https://github.com/awesome-chatgpt-prompts/awesome-chatgpt-prompts-github/blob/fb74a836b001df1dc7229b9d7a7baf993c32b519/README.md) inform level-fitting items, explicit response choices and learner-centered materials. The source's ESL instruction to omit explanations is intentionally not adopted: practical buyers need enough instructions to finish the exercise. The completed-example, blank-workspace and answer checks above are this app's requirements.

## Visual Design

- Use a welcoming, topic-appropriate workbook identity with clear exercise headings, readable instructions and genuinely usable writing or calculation space.
- Keep a worked example close to its matching blank exercise while making the difference unmistakable. Use native fields, tables and checkboxes that support the task, rather than decorative boxes or unnecessary illustrations.
- Inspect actual pages for writing room, intact exercises, answer separation and balanced page breaks. Preserve full instructions and editable structures; slide word and whitespace limits do not apply.

## Final Quality Review

- Complete each exercise from its supplied inputs and instructions; check worked examples, blank fields, answer criteria and later handoffs agree.
- Inspect the actual workbook for adequate response space, legible labels/tables and page breaks that keep each exercise usable; fix safe layout defects without altering approved exercises or answers.
- Flag missing teaching or unusable tasks instead of inventing the creator's method. Keep grounded optional improvements in the shared review report, outside the buyer workbook.
