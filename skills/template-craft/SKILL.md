# Template Craft

## Planning Contract

For outline planning only, preserve the promised templates and use order. Identify the complete copy-ready item, necessary placeholders, filled example and important adaptation or handoff. Do not draft templates, invent companion products or turn the kit into a course.

## Production Contract

Deliver each promised reusable template in a form the buyer can copy and use. Clearly label fields and placeholders and explain which parts to replace, retain or remove. Give a complete illustrative filled example that matches the blank original.

Explain when to use each template and how related templates pass information between them. Avoid making the buyer enter the same information repeatedly. Include a realistic variation or difficult case so adaptation goes beyond changing names.

Keep customer-facing wording separate from instructions to the template user. Preserve tables, formulas, code and message structures when they are part of the usable asset. A list of suggested templates is not the kit. Do not invent private details, legal assurances or creator results. Preserve approved originals outside the authorized edit.

Use distinctive, consistent placeholder names such as [Session Date], not ambiguous pronouns or real-looking private data. Mark optional passages and explain the condition for retaining them. The example must fill every required field and demonstrate the same structure as the blank original; do not silently add fields that the buyer cannot find. Avoid hidden dependencies such as an attachment or policy absent from the kit.

Where templates form a workflow, give their use order and one short handoff example showing which information carries forward. Include the difficult variation that matters to this niche, such as a late change or missing response, rather than padding with interchangeable messages. Keep the creator's actual boundaries and method in the copy; do not promise outcomes or services they did not offer.

Within the writing turn, copy the blank template mentally, replace its required fields and compare it to the filled example. Resolve dangling placeholders, contradictory dates, unusable table fields and instruction text that could accidentally be sent to a customer. This is an internal finish check, not another user approval or a separate generation pass.

## Reference Basis

Original adaptation of complete, usable examples and context-sensitive instructions from [h4x3rotab's technical-docs-writing skill](https://gist.github.com/h4x3rotab/5a08422abad6093359da2c696a97a265), and example correctness/scoped revisions from [ai-boost's technical_writer prompt](https://github.com/ai-boost/awesome-prompts/blob/main/prompts/technical_writer.txt). No developer-only output schema or fixed length is required.

## Visual Design

<!-- Modified 2026-11-02: design guidance adapted from Anthropic skills (Apache-2.0), pinned at ca45fc496a3436251f191354091d557da31c15b5. See THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md. -->

- Create a coherent kit identity with clear template names and easy-to-copy native blocks or tables. Visually separate the buyer’s instructions, blank originals and filled examples.
- Keep placeholders recognizable and editable, with enough writing room where needed. Use spacing, hierarchy and restrained accents rather than turning each sentence into a colored panel.
- Inspect real page breaks and copy boundaries: instructions must not look like text to send to a customer, and the filled example must visibly match its blank original. Presentation limits do not apply.

## Final Quality Review

- Fill the blank original using its own instructions and compare it with the completed example: required fields, optional passages and handoffs must match.
- Inspect actual editable tables, copy-ready message boundaries and page breaks so buyer instructions cannot be mistaken for customer-facing copy; correct safe formatting defects.
- Keep approved original wording intact. Flag missing dependencies or substantive contradictions, and suggest only grounded optional improvements through the shared review report.
