# Warm Dimension

Read ../product-page-design/references/slide-art-direction.md. Adapt Rizza's deliberate source-to-visual workflow without copying her identity or personal facts.

Use generous modern type, warm white, deep cobalt and a restrained apricot accent unless the creator supplies a palette. Give illustrations coherent soft light, thoughtful layering and mature material detail. Paper depth is a treatment; decorative dishes, arches and book stacks are not default subjects.

Represent the actual lesson. A website journey can become dimensional pages, actions and destinations. A comparison can show the same guide reaching distinct outcomes. Match exact source details to visible features and include that mapping in the image prompt. Generate no audience wording inside the image.

Resolve the learner's question and headline clues before choosing objects. Material depth should clarify the source's action or relationship: a submitted form reaches a collection point, confirmed payment precedes access, or the same input has visibly different optional outcomes. Use the few specific objects needed for that explanation. Do not turn every topic into a book stack, doorway, shopping bag or staged still life. Keep digital delivery visibly digital, omit all letters and numbers, and align editable labels to the illustration's actual arrangement.

Choose each composition separately: a broad illustrated relationship, asymmetric demonstration, central decision or clear comparison. Integrate the substantial image with its background and labels. Avoid small floating pictures and repeated left-copy/right-image pages.

Every title uses Title Case. The title and all labels together stay at 14 words or fewer; full original content and explanation stay in presenter notes. Preserve at least 40% quiet space. A prompt or successful export is not visual acceptance. Verify actual native text, original artwork and per-slide notes before claiming delivery.


Create the three actual editable illustrated samples in Canva; follow the shared native sample review contract. Reuse this slide's unchanged approved original artwork first. Prepare only missing final-use illustrations in the same direction, then transfer the real images with separately editable text, supporting geometry and full notes. Do not replace the illustrated direction with an icon-only fallback or insert a local mockup approval stage.
