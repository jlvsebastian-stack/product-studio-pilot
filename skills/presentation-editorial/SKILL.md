# Quiet Editorial

Read ../product-page-design/references/slide-art-direction.md. Adapt Rizza's deliberate source-to-visual workflow without copying her identity or personal facts.

Use expressive display serif headings with a clear sans, fine charcoal line work and restrained washes. Start with the creator's palette; otherwise use warm ivory, deep ink and precise vermilion. Keep the treatment mature, with deliberate scale and confident whitespace.

Illustrate what this slide teaches. Website choices need recognizable pages, visitor actions and distinct destinations. Real sequences show actual steps; alternatives remain independent. A tree, envelope or gate alone does not explain a website. Record exact source anchors and their visible counterparts before writing the image prompt.

Use the learner's question to decide what the ink drawing must make understandable. Make the action legible through specific objects and relationships; use restrained washes to separate a meaningful contrast or destination, not to decorate empty space. Headline clues and source details decide the subject. Three alternatives can share a visual baseline while their different outcomes remain distinct; a causal change needs visible before/after evidence. Avoid interchangeable desks, leaves, envelopes and arrows. Keep lettering out of the illustration and position separate editable words against its actual rows or focal regions.

Compose broad narrative illustrations, a central annotated relationship, sparse comparisons or an open decision layout. Keep artwork substantial and wording separately editable. Inspect the opening and demanding slide together for coherent art treatment and visibly different compositions.

Every title uses Title Case. The title and all labels together stay at 14 words or fewer; full original content and explanation stay in presenter notes. Preserve at least 40% quiet space. A prompt or successful export is not visual acceptance. Verify actual native text, original artwork and per-slide notes before claiming delivery.


Create the three actual editable illustrated samples in Canva; follow the shared native sample review contract. Reuse this slide's unchanged approved original artwork first. Prepare only missing final-use illustrations in the same direction, then transfer the real images with separately editable text, supporting geometry and full notes. Do not replace the illustrated direction with an icon-only fallback or insert a local mockup approval stage.
