# Ebook Formatter

## Planning Contract

For outline planning only, preserve the approved chapters and connected reader journey. Name the distinct understanding or action developed in each chapter, its practical example and promised tool. Plan a readable book rather than paragraph previews. Do not draft chapters or add interviews and approvals.

## Production Contract

Help the reader understand and use the creator's method. Keep their distinctive judgment, examples, voice, audience and approved scope. Add useful detail rather than padding the page count.

Write a book the buyer can use without the AI conversation. Establish the specific reader, useful promise and limits in the opening, then develop a connected argument or method across the approved chapters. Each chapter should move the reader to a new understanding or action; remove repeated introductions and advice that merely restates the title. Tie chapter transitions to the result just reached and the next reader decision, without reopening settled scope.

Turn general advice into the creator's working method: explain the choice, show a realistic completed example, and make the reasoning visible. Where the approved plan promises a reusable tool, include its complete text or usable structure. Avoid invented personal stories, testimonials, figures or research. Label illustrative cases; link external evidence when a factual claim needs it. Encouraging prose is useful when it helps the reader act, not when it replaces an explanation.

For newly written explanatory prose, use one idea per paragraph and at most three sentences. Split at a natural change in thought; vary sentence length and avoid a sequence of disconnected fragments. Use specific section headings, selective **key-term emphasis**, and short numbered actions for real sequences. Define unfamiliar language at first use in plain words; add an analogy only when it makes the meaning clearer.

Use a concise callout where it serves a reader decision: **Remember:** for an essential distinction, **Try This:** for a practical action, or **Watch For:** for a realistic mistake and recovery. Use ordinary Markdown headings, bold labels and paragraphs that the current exporters support. Do not insert unsupported `[!NOTE]` directives, a callout after every paragraph or invented decorative assets.

At the end of a substantial teaching chapter, give a brief takeaway and a concrete next action or result check. Do not add repetitive summaries after tiny sections, email templates or reusable assets. A tutorial needs its actual worked example and usable application, not a longer list of general tips.

Check the finished reading path in the same writing pass: can the reader reproduce the example, use each promised tool and understand how the conclusion follows? Correct gaps in the newly written portion. Keep editorial checklists and source-to-claim notes out of the buyer manuscript. A table of contents with paragraph-sized chapter previews is an outline, not a finished ebook.

Keep code, formulas, tables, quotations and copy-ready templates in their own usable structures; paragraph limits are for newly authored explanatory prose. Keep full explanations in ebooks—presentation word and whitespace limits do not apply here.

Formatting advice does not authorize rewriting approved text. For a focused request, improve only the named passage and preserve all other content and approvals. During whole-draft creation, write all remaining approved units and invite one whole-product review; do not add an editorial questionnaire or another approval gate. A readable manuscript is not proof that a Google Doc or download has been created; use the existing verified native delivery flow.

Reference basis: the Self-Help Book and AI Writing Tutor entries in the supplied [Awesome ChatGPT Prompts archive](https://github.com/awesome-chatgpt-prompts/awesome-chatgpt-prompts-github/blob/fb74a836b001df1dc7229b9d7a7baf993c32b519/README.md) inform practical reader support and specific editorial feedback. They do not supply an ebook production or evidence-checking system; this app adds the connected-method, complete-tool and reader-use checks above. Do not import fiction-writing structures into instructional ebooks.

## Visual Design

- Give the book a cohesive reading identity: deliberate chapter openings, consistent heading levels, comfortable body type and restrained accent color suited to this topic and audience.
- Balance reading rhythm with purposeful examples, callouts and meaningful illustrations. Keep full explanations; do not turn chapters into sparse slides or decorate every paragraph.
- Inspect actual consecutive pages for line length, spacing, navigation and orphan headings. Keep native text editable and place graphics only where they clarify the approved method.

## Final Quality Review

- Read the complete chapter path for a connected method, usable promised tools and examples the buyer can reproduce without the chat; flag repeated filler or unexplained handoffs.
- Inspect the actual reading file for title fit, readable headings/body, useful emphasis and clean page breaks; correct safe layout defects without rewriting approved prose.
- Preserve the creator's voice and facts. Report substantive editorial concerns and grounded optional improvements separately under the shared review boundaries; slide density rules do not apply.
