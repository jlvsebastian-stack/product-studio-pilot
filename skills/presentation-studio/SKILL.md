# Signal Studio

Read ../product-page-design/references/slide-art-direction.md. Adapt Rizza's deliberate source-to-visual workflow without copying her identity or personal facts.

Use condensed display type, a readable sans, deep midnight and focused lime/cyan accents unless the creator supplies a palette. Establish technical confidence through clear relationships and exact alignment. Gadgets, pipes, glowing circuits and machinery are not a costume for a digital topic.

The image shows the taught mechanism. Website teaching needs recognizable visitor actions, form submissions or payment-confirmation/access relationships. Physical cutaways suit actual physical mechanisms; comparisons suit alternatives. Record source anchors and how each becomes visible in the generated graphic.

Start with the learner's question and headline clues, then describe the exact mechanism that answers it. Contrast an input and outcome, reveal a genuine dependency, or make independent alternatives comparable. Use purposeful diagram scale and source-specific objects; a digital transaction needs a digital destination, not an invented pipe, engine or parcel. Directional marks communicate an actual sequence or dependency only. Keep the graphic free of letters and numbers, and make the later editable labels match its real lanes and endpoints.

Vary between a dominant explanatory graphic, a wide comparison, a split demonstration and a focused decision. Keep images substantial with separately editable labels. A generic switch or tiny dark thumbnail is unfinished even when it fits.

Every title uses Title Case. The title and all labels together stay at 14 words or fewer; full original content and explanation stay in presenter notes. Preserve at least 40% quiet space. A prompt or successful export is not visual acceptance. Verify actual native text, original artwork and per-slide notes before claiming delivery.


Create the three actual editable illustrated samples in Canva; follow the shared native sample review contract. Reuse this slide's unchanged approved original artwork first. Prepare only missing final-use illustrations in the same direction, then transfer the real images with separately editable text, supporting geometry and full notes. Do not replace the illustrated direction with an icon-only fallback or insert a local mockup approval stage.
