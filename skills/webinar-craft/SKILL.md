# Webinar Craft

## Planning Contract

Keep the approved sequence. Describe the useful learner outcome, demonstration inputs and result, practice/answer check, and presenter-note role for each planned beat. Include realistic timing only if duration is known. Plan an actual slide presentation with notes, not a script alone; add no new slides, interview questions or approvals.

## Production Contract

Build an experience the presenter can deliver. Establish the learner's useful outcome, connect the teaching beats, demonstrate the method and include the agreed practice or audience decisions. Offer a recap and practical next step within the approved outline; do not invent additional slides or a sales pitch.

Every slide needs its own presenter notes explaining what to say, show, demonstrate or ask. Include the expected response or success check for planned activities and a useful transition. Add pacing guidance only when appropriate to an agreed duration. Keep substantial explanations and facilitation instructions in notes.

Make the demonstration deliverable: state its starting inputs, perform the actual steps, show the expected result, explain one likely mistake and give a useful recovery or verbal fallback if a live tool fails. For an audience question, include the intended answer and why it is right in the notes; do not leave the presenter to invent the teaching. A worked example should let a beginner repeat the method using their own inputs. Separate an illustrative calculation from a promised business result.

Use natural, supportive spoken language. Include a short invitation to ask for clarification where it helps, without forcing an activity after every slide. Acknowledge likely uncertainty and give a concrete next action. Reuse the agreed scope and duration; do not append sales slides, hidden backup slides, a rehearsal questionnaire or new approvals. Check the current draft's teaching completeness silently in the same response.

Use slide-deck-architect and the selected visual direction for the actual slides, including the 14-word visible total and 40% empty-space rule. Deliver an editable Canva presentation with native notes through the existing verified production flow. A script alone is not the webinar presentation. Hosting, recording and live delivery remain separate actions.

Reference adaptations: [ai-boost's Conference Talk Slide Outline Builder](https://github.com/ai-boost/awesome-prompts/blob/main/library/en/business.md) supplies the useful demo setup/success/fallback and realistic pacing patterns. The [Awesome ChatGPT Prompts fork's Educational Content Creator and Public Speaking Coach](https://github.com/awesome-chatgpt-prompts/awesome-chatgpt-prompts-github#act-as-a-public-speaking-coach) supply learner engagement and audience-aware delivery intent, not evidence of instructional quality. These brief role prompts are starting ideas; the complete demonstration, answers and native-note checks above are this app's output contract.

- Use the selected presentation concept in the real Canva deck: Title Case titles, at most14 total visible words and at least40% empty space, with the teaching explanation in native notes.
- Give each demonstration, comparison or practice beat a purposeful composition and source-specific graphic. Keep labels close to their subjects and maintain clear scale and contrast during screen sharing.
- Inspect the actual three samples and final slides with notes, including difficult teaching beats. Preserve approved visuals and full notes; no extra raster mockup, theme selection round or script-only delivery.

## Final Quality Review

- Rehearse the finished teaching path from native slides and their notes: demonstrations need starting inputs, actions, expected results and a useful recovery; activities need their intended answer/check.
- Inspect actual Canva text, explanatory artwork, labels and every slide's notes with slide-deck-architect's 14-word and 40% whitespace limits. A script or thumbnail alone is not presenter-ready proof.
- Correct safe authorized layout defects only; preserve approved wording, notes and artwork without new art generation. Report substantive delivery concerns and grounded optional improvements under the shared review boundaries.
