# The AI handles the research

First-time creators should not have to know competing offers or collect evidence links. In the reported stage5 case, the AI had already inspected educational offers and recorded their contents, but the code rejected the whole check because optional prices/reviews contained UNRUN. It replaced useful replies with the same demand for two links, even after the creator said they did not know.

Keep this a focused fix inside the current local app: distinguish unperformed research from unknown optional details, reuse inspected examples and the creator's improvement direction, and let the AI explain comparable products in plain language. Do not invent prices, demand or exclusive advantages; an initial comparison is not sales validation. If a real lookup fails, explain it supportively and take ownership of retrying instead of assigning competitor homework.

Make the creation partner warm, cheerful and practical. Preserve useful natural model replies when they already meet the baseline/purpose rules; reserve deterministic fallback copy for actual blocking gaps or overprobing. Encourage based on what the creator supplied, without canned praise or invented results. Keep existing preferences, model choice, approvals, source boundaries and saved projects. No research platform, questionnaire modes, hosted services or KKY work.

Prove the partial-price/review failure before and after, whole-check unverified truth, beginner reassurance and nonrepetition, then verify a real stage5 response and recover the creator's current blocked conversation through normal controls. One independent review and proportionate combined acceptance; retain unchanged provider/export evidence.
